SHOGO VR  -  play Shogo: Mobile Armor Division in a VR headset
==============================================================
An unofficial VR mod, unaffiliated with Monolith Productions or any of its
affiliates and subsidiaries. Free. Created by the people in AUTHORS.txt.

WHAT YOU NEED
- Shogo: Mobile Armor Division (Steam or GOG), version 2.2.
- A PC VR headset with SteamVR, and SteamVR set as the OpenXR runtime:
  SteamVR > Settings > OpenXR > "Set SteamVR as OpenXR Runtime".
- Shogo running in a window. With dgVoodoo 2, set its appearance to
  "Windowed" in dgVoodooCpl. (The VR mod takes care of the window's size.)

SETTINGS
- In the game: Options > vr settings. Stick up/down picks a setting,
  left/right changes it. Changes apply immediately.
- On the desktop: the launcher's VR Settings tab ("Shogo VR Settings" in
  the Start menu opens it directly). It also applies straight away.
- Options include left- or right-handed, aim with hand or head, two-handed
  aiming, which way the stick walks you, snap or smooth turning, comfort
  vignette, 6DoF, stereo depth, your body, HUD size and distance, menu
  screen size, gun size, sharpening and more.

THE GUN IN YOUR HAND
- You hold the detailed first-person gun with Sanjuro's arms, as in the
  original game. The settings can hide the arms ("gun arms"), or switch to
  the simpler third-person gun model ("gun model").
- The parts of each gun model are listed in ShogoVR_models.txt in the
  ShogoVR folder. If arms still show on some gun, send that file in.

TWO-HANDED AIMING AND YOUR BODY
- Hold your other hand out in front of the gun hand, along the barrel, and
  the gun aims from your gun hand towards it - like holding a rifle.
- Look down to see your body (Sanjuro, or your mech). It walks, runs,
  crouches and jumps as you move. Its head and arms are hidden: the game
  engine can't bend a model's arms to follow your controllers, so your
  hands are the gun in your hand. "VRBodyInfo" in the console lists the
  model's parts if something that should be hidden isn't.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- Logs in the ShogoVR folder record what happened and when:
  ShogoVR_launch.log (what the Shogo VR shortcut did),
  ShogoVRBridge.log (window, focus, capture, settings) and
  ShogoVR_game.log (player mode, transforms, camera FOV, zoom, resolution,
  renderer resets). Both start fresh each run - send them in with any bug
  report before playing again.

PLAYING
- Start "Shogo VR" from your Desktop or Start menu and press Play. It
  starts the headset bridge and the game, and closes the bridge when you
  quit Shogo. The launcher's VR Settings tab has all the VR options.
- Or start Shogo any way you normally do (Shogo.exe, Steam's Play button)
  with -rez ShogoVR on its command line (Shogo.exe: Advanced). The mod
  starts the headset bridge by itself and closes it when you quit - no need
  to start the bridge yourself.
- If the "Shogo VR" shortcut doesn't start the game on your PC, start Shogo
  once from its own launcher with -rez ShogoVR: the mod remembers exactly
  how the game was started, and the shortcut uses that from then on.
- Put on the headset, look straight ahead and click the left thumbstick
  (or press Ctrl+Shift+R) to recentre.
- Keep the Shogo window visible on the desktop while you play.

CONTROLS (Quest / Rift layout - Index, Vive and WMR are similar)
  Aim .................... right hand        Fire ............... right trigger
  Move ................... left stick        Turn ............... right stick left/right
  Change weapon .......... right stick up/down, or right grip
  Jump ................... A                 Crouch ............. B (hold)
  Transform (mech) ....... tap B (in a mech, holding B crouches)
  Menu ................... left menu button  Mission log ........ X (hold)
  Weapon list ............ Y or left grip     Recentre ........... left stick click
  Ladders ................ stick forward climbs up, back climbs down
  In menus: sticks move, A/trigger selects, B goes back.
  Transforming presses the key bound to "Vehicle mode toggle" in Options >
  Keyboard for you, so that needs a key. The tractor beam is still on the
  keyboard.
  Left-handed mode (in Settings) mirrors all of this: left hand aims and
  fires, right stick moves.
  You can remap buttons in SteamVR's controller binding settings.

BEST PICTURE
- Shogo VR takes the picture straight from the game's renderer (when the
  game runs through dgVoodoo), so your monitor's resolution no longer
  limits it: pick a high resolution in Shogo, e.g. 3840x2160 (with NVIDIA
  DSR enabled, those resolutions appear even if your desktop stays 1080p).
  The Shogo window may then be bigger than your screen - that's expected.
- A "left eye" window on the desktop shows what your left eye sees, flat,
  with the HUD - record that with OBS ("Window Capture"). Ctrl+Shift+M
  shows/hides it.
- Shogo VR sets dgVoodoo's emulated video memory to 2 GB (its default
  256 MB is too little at VR resolutions and makes textures blurrier the
  longer you play). Up to 4 GB can be chosen in Shogo VR Settings; if the
  game misbehaves at 3 or 4 GB, go back to 2 GB. Your original
  dgVoodoo.conf is kept as dgVoodoo.conf.shogovr-backup.
- Run Shogo at the highest resolution your desktop supports. Each eye gets
  half of the game window.
- Even better: run your desktop at 3840x2160 with NVIDIA DSR (NVIDIA Control
  Panel > Manage 3D settings > DSR - Factors > 4.00x) or AMD Virtual Super
  Resolution, and choose 3840x2160 in Shogo. At that resolution the HUD is
  also shown as its own crisp panel.
- Bridge hotkeys: Ctrl+Shift+S sharpening, Ctrl+Shift+U upscaling,
  Ctrl+Shift+F fix the game window, Ctrl+Shift+D fix Windows display scaling.

USEFUL CONSOLE SETTINGS (open the console with ~; put + in front to save,
e.g. "+VRHudWidth 65")
  VRHudWidth ........ HUD width in degrees (default 60) - spreads it out
  VRHudDepth ........ HUD distance in metres (default 3.5)
  VRMenuWidth ....... size of the floating screen for menus (default 80)
  VRSnapTurn ........ degrees per snap turn, 0 = smooth turning (default 45)
  VRGunScale ........ size of arms/gun in your hand on foot (default 1.5)
  VRCalibrate ....... (command) stand on flat ground to set the world scale
  VRRecenter ........ (command) make your current facing "straight ahead"
  VRInfo ............ (command) show the current VR state

OPTIONS FOR THE LAUNCHER
Extra Shogo command-line options can go in ShogoVR.ini (in the
ShogoVR folder):
  [Launch]
  ExtraArgs=+DisableMovies 1

UNINSTALLING
Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > "Shogo VR", or run
"ShogoVR.exe --uninstall". Shogo itself is never changed.
